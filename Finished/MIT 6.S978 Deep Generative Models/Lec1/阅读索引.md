## 必读

| 论文 | 目录 | 一句话 |
|---|---|---|
| Zoran & Weiss, ICCV 2011 | [From Learning Models…](From%20Learning%20Models%20of%20Natural%20Image%20Patches%20to%20Whole%20Image%20Restoration/) | EPLL：用 patch 先验整图复原；GMM + Half-Quadratic Splitting |
| Zoran & Weiss, NeurIPS 2012 | [Natural Images, Gaussian Mixtures and Dead Leaves](Natural%20Images,%20Gaussian%20Mixtures%20and%20Dead%20Leaves/) | 简单 GMM 为何强；Dead Leaves / 遮挡解释 |
| Ulyanov et al., CVPR 2018 | [Deep Image Prior](Deep%20Image%20Prior/) | 未训练 ConvNet 作图像先验；早停抗噪 |

每篇文件夹含：`*.pdf`、`中文总结.md`、`中文详解-逐节梳理.md`。
