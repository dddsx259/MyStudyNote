        Slip Days (Late Submission) Policies
        This 'Slip Days Policies' only applies to the programming assignments and problem-set assignments.

        Each assignment has a due date and a due time, which will be posted on the course web page. For Prog & Prob-set assignments, we will use a system of "slip days" to give you some flexibility with the assignment deadlines. Each person starts the term with five slip days, which can be used to push back assignment deadlines. Slip days work as follows:
        • Pushing an assignment deadline back by one day (24 hours) costs one slip day.
        • An assignment deadline can be pushed back at most three days.
        • Partial slip days are not allowed, e.g., it is not possible to use part of a slip day to push a deadline back by six hours.
        • Slip days are not transferable from one student to another.
        • By default, the slip days will be applied automatically* as is needed according to the exact timestamp of submission.
        • A FREE late window of 30 minutes is allowed to accommodate potential technical issues upon submission. In other words, submissions made within 30 minutes after the deadline need no slip days to cover them. (Note: this 30 minutes buffer only applies to immediately after the deadline, not applies to after the 1st or 2nd slip day.)
        • All the thresholds will be observed strictly. For example, a submission that is late for 1801 seconds (i.e., 30 minutes plus 1 second) will use one slip day.
        • Assignments that are submitted late (with no slip days to cover them) and are within 3-day late will be graded but with a penalty of 20% per uncovered day. It is your responsibility to keep track of your slip-day count. We shall apply the above policy if we find that you have exhausted your slip-day credits and submitted an assignment late.

        * Let D be the deadline, and T your submission timestamp. The number of slip days that will be used for a submission, noted as SD, is calculated as follows:

        if (T - D) <= 30 minutes, SD = 0;
        else if (T - D) <= 24 hours, SD = 1;
        else if (T - D) <= 48 hours, SD = 2;
        else if (T - D) <= 72 hours, SD = 3;
        else SD = 0 & mark = 0.
