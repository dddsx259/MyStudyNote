## Course Learning Outcomes

1. Appreciate and describe the beauty, the utility, and the "Way" of mathematics
2. Comprehend and describe how mathematics was and is being developed as a work of human culture
3. Investigate and describe the interplay between mathematics and other areas of human culture
4. Investigate and explain the role of mathematics in the development of civilization

## Assignment 1 — Group Project Plan

- 字数: 200–400 words
- 内容: 暂定标题与选题动机; 组员姓名 UID 与 tutorial group; 书/作品与 primary source + 参考文献; **central theme** 与课程目标; 简述 essay 内容与 CLO 关联
- 截止: **2026-10-16 16:55**; 可根据反馈更新至 **2026-10-29 16:55**
- 每组一人提交; 迟交惩罚阶梯见题面
- 可用 generative AI, 但组员须理解内容并能答问

## Assignment 2 — Mathematical Articles (大众媒体剪报)

- 收集 **10** 则与数学**显式**相关的大众媒体文章 (英文或中文), 出版不早于 **2026-09-18/19**
- 排除: 专业数学科普站/学术向媒体/个人博客/仅隐含数学
- 每则: 首页图, 全文可及性, 来源与日期, 英文说明为何符合标准及与 CLO 的联系
- 截止: **2026-11-30 16:55**

## Group Project (Essay + Presentation)

- 2–3 人一组, **须同一 tutorial group** (presentation 在 tut 进行)
- Essay: 2000–3000 words (不含图与 bibliography); 基于数学相关书籍/电影等, 非单纯书评, 须有解读与课程主题关联
- Presentation: 见 `02-presentation-grade-descriptors.pdf` (theme, content, knowledge, analysis, delivery, teamwork, Q&A)

## Central Theme 提示

见 `03-central-theme-examples.pdf`: 发现 vs 发明, Calculus 影响, Good Will Hunting, Imitation Game 等示例 — 须有清晰中心论点贯穿全文.
