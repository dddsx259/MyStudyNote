## A. 必须先改 (形式 / 完整性)

### 1. 补全 Tutorial 占位符

- **问题**: `Tutorial: [tutor, group, day and time].` 仍是模板占位符, 两份稿子都没填.
- **风险**: 形式不完整, 可能直接扣格式分.
- **改法**: 填实际信息, 例如 `Tutorial: [Tutor Name], Group [N], [Day] [HH:MM]–[HH:MM].`
- **位置**: 成员栏同一行末尾.

### 2. Figure 1 不要丢

- **问题**: 原 docx 里有一张论证图 (Figure 1), `plan.txt` 纯文字稿里没有.
- **图的内容** (从 docx alt text 读出):
  - 左支: Cantor 的区分 — Transfinite (可数学判定 / 可被超越) vs Absolute Infinite (属于上帝 / 超出数学判定)
  - 右支: 「哪些进一步主张成立?」— Franzelin: 澄清后的概念在宗教上无可非议, 但神的善不要求必然创造; Gutberlet: 上帝知道整个无限序列, 但知道可能性不要求创造它们
  - 底部结论: Accepting a concept leaves its consequences open to debate.
- **价值**: 这张图把你们的核心论点「接受概念 ≠ 接受推论」一眼说清, 是加分项.
- **改法**: 最终排版稿保留 Figure 1, 并在正文「Main work and central theme」末尾加一句引用, 如 `Our argument is summarised in Figure 1.`
- **注意**: 若最终交纯文字版, 至少把图的结论句写进正文.

## B. 建议改 (论点与结构)

### 3. 动机句写得更具体

- **原文**: `We chose this topic because Cantor's distinction between mathematical and divine infinity could ease religious concerns while leaving further disagreements unresolved.`
- **问题**: `ease religious concerns` / `further disagreements` 偏抽象, 读者不知道具体是哪种顾虑, 哪种分歧.
- **改法**: 点明具体对象. 例如:
  - `… could ease concerns about pantheism, while leaving open whether divine knowledge entails creation.`
- **收益**: 一句话就让评分者看到你们知道争论在哪.

### 4. 中心论点再聚焦 (Franzelin vs Gutberlet 的主次)

- **原文**: `… the theologians assessed particular claims through their views of divine perfection: Franzelin defended freedom in creation; Gutberlet defended divine knowledge.`
- **问题**: 两条线并列, 200–400 词内容易写散, 像两个人物小传.
- **两种改法 (二选一, 写清)**:
  - **主线 + 对照**: 以 Gutberlet 的 π 论证为主线 (更具体, 有「上帝知道 π 的每一位」这个抓手), Franzelin 作对照. 例如: `We focus on Gutberlet's argument that God knows every digit of pi, using Franzelin's defence of freedom in creation as a contrast.`
  - **明确说对比本身就是论点**: `Our contribution is the comparison itself: the same distinction was accepted for different theological reasons.`
- **收益**: 避免「两个人各写一段」的松散结构.

### 5. 突出你们的贡献句

- **原文**: `… recognising infinite objects of knowledge need not entail an infinite creation.`
- **问题**: 这是全文最关键的一句, 却埋在长句中间.
- **改法**: 单独成句, 或用强调. 例如:
  - `Our central claim: recognising infinite objects of knowledge need not entail an infinite creation.`
- **收益**: 评分者一眼看到你们的论点, 而不是只看到史料复述.

### 6. CLO 对应写得更实

- **原文**: `This addresses CLO 2 through mathematics as a human cultural activity and CLO 3 through its interaction with theology.`
- **问题**: 只点了 CLO 编号, 没说**怎么做**才算对应.
- **改法**: 各加半句具体做法. 例如:
  - CLO 2: `… we treat Cantor's distinction as a human response to a cultural-religious problem, not only a technical definition.`
  - CLO 3: `… we show how theological assumptions (divine perfection) shaped the reception of a mathematical concept.`
- **收益**: CLO 对应从「贴标签」变成「有论证」.

## C. 可选润色 (篇幅 / 引用 / 措辞)

### 8. 参考文献格式统一

- **问题**: Aquinas 一条缺出版信息: `Aquinas, T. Summa Theologiae, I, q. 7. Primary source.`
- **改法**: 补版本或写通用说明, 例如:
  - `Aquinas, T. Summa Theologiae, I, q. 7 (any standard edition or translation). Primary source.`
- **提醒**: 若课程要求 APA / Chicago, 四条统一成同一格式; Dauben 与 Gutberlet 已较规范, 主要补 Aquinas.

### 9. 措辞与语法小修

- `Why could readers accept a concept but reject what its author inferred from it?` — 可保留 (设问有力), 但若想更正式可改 `Why might readers accept a concept yet reject the conclusions its author drew from it?`
- `using Aquinas as historical background` — 可改 `with Aquinas as historical background` 更顺.
- `Our conclusions will concern these documented exchanges.` — 可改 `Our conclusions will be limited to these documented exchanges.` (强调不超出史料, 更严谨).
